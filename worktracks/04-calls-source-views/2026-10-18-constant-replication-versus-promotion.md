Replicating a checked owned nominal graph into fresh construction guards need not assert Copy or Drop absence: each fresh result goes through ordinary cleanup and Drop selection. Its private template is not a runtime guest instance and is owned by the same never-retired anchor as compiled constant data. Never infer NoDrop from this eligibility; existing global Drop/promotion completeness remains held.
