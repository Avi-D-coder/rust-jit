## Checked producer and consumer

The new producer independently reconstructs the pinned runtime catalogue for that exact selected definition, requires its kind Panic, reparses the actual invocation at the exact body/range, compares the regenerated NoItem proof, and validates the actual operand contract through lower_static_panic.
